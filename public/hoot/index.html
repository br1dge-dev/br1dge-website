THE ASSEMBLY DESK

A little knight.
Piece by piece.

Everything you need to bring Hoot into your game. Start with a complete figure as your reference, then fit the separate parts to your rig.

  1. 01

    Build the silhouette

    Keep the head large, the body compact and the visible limbs short. Overlap the head above the body band; tuck shoulder and ankle tabs behind the torso.

  2. 02

    Equip the little knight

    Attach the sword to the right wing, with the grip under the fingers. Place the left wing behind the shield. The sword should be about as long as the whole standing owl.

  3. 03

    Line up each expression

    Align the beak, eye line and helmet centre before swapping heads. Set scale, overlap, draw order and pivots in your animation system.

✦

One small refinement before close-ups

The left toes are slightly thicker than the right. Match the pair before close-up animation, and check the subtle outline differences between individual parts.

Character & proportions +

Preserve the white, hand-painted cardboard, open silver helmet, orange beak and feet, simple body band, brown strap and single round brass buckle. Keep the shield plain brown.

The larger eye is always on Hoot’s anatomical right. Use the complete armed figures to match proportions and equipment placement. Each component file contains only its named part.

Files, transparency & rig setup +

The kit contains 18 native-resolution PNGs: four complete armed figures, seven heads, five body parts, one sword and one shield. These are individual source images, not a registered animation atlas or a prebuilt rig.

Use the original PNG transparency directly; no green screen is needed. Keep transparent edge padding when packing an atlas. Some foreground pixels have alpha around 253/255; colours hidden beneath fully transparent pixels should stay invisible in your renderer.

Nothing has been upscaled. Every gallery card shows the native dimensions; the manifest also contains SHA-256 checksums.
